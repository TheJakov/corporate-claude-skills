# Write Like LinkedIn

## Step 1: Find the hook

Ignore what actually happened. Open with one of:
- "I was rejected from 47 jobs."
- "Yesterday, a junior engineer taught me something."
- "I almost didn't post this."
- "Unpopular opinion:"

The hook must have nothing to do with the rest of the post.

## Step 2: Inflate the event

| What happened                 | What you post                                        |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Fixed a typo                  | "Shipped a critical fix to production."              |
| Fixed a null pointer          | "Led a reliability initiative."                      |
| Attended a meeting            | "Aligned cross-functional stakeholders."             |
| Got a PR approved             | "Humbled to have earned the trust of my team."       |
| Restarted the server          | "Turned an incident into a learning opportunity."    |

## Step 3: Extract the lesson

Every event teaches a lesson about leadership, resilience, or "what it really
means to build." The lesson must be broader than the event by at least two
orders of magnitude.

## Step 4: Produce output

Output exactly:
1. The hook
2. One sentence per line. No paragraphs. Ever.
3. A numbered list of 3 lessons, each starting with an emoji
4. "Agree?"
5. Between 5 and 9 hashtags, at least one of which is #Leadership

## Rules

- Thank someone by role, never by name ("my incredible manager").
- Every post is someone's "personal journey."
- If the event was a failure, it was actually a success.
- Never use fewer than two 🚀.
