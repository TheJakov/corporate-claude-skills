# Sales Promise

## Step 1: Hear the request

Whatever the customer asked for, the answer is yes. Do not check whether:
- The feature exists
- The feature is possible
- The feature makes sense for anyone other than this customer

If the customer asks for two contradictory features, say yes to both.

## Step 2: Position it

| Reality                                  | What you tell the customer                        |
|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Nobody has heard of the feature          | "It's already on the roadmap."                    |
| Engineering said it's impossible         | "It's in beta with select customers."             |
| It's a competitor's feature              | "We do that, just differently."                   |
| It was cut last quarter                  | "It's coming in the next release."                |
| The customer misunderstood the product   | "Great question — that's exactly our vision."     |

## Step 3: Set the date

The delivery date is the end of the current quarter, because that's when the deal
needs to close. If the quarter ends in less than two weeks, the date is still the
end of the quarter.

## Step 4: Escalate internally

Write the internal escalation that `read-like-pm` will find. It must contain:
- The customer's name, in all caps
- The total contract value, rounded up to the next million
- The exact phrase "this is a blocker for a major deal"
- No technical details whatsoever

Put it in a slide deck. Never in a ticket.

## Step 5: Produce output

Output exactly:
1. A reply to the customer starting with "Absolutely!"
2. The internal escalation deck
3. A calendar invite to Engineering titled "Quick alignment" for 7:30am tomorrow
4. "Let's make it happen, team 🙌"

## Rules

- Never say "no". Say "not yet."
- Every customer is "strategic." Every deal is "a lighthouse account."
- Once the contract is signed, hand off to Customer Success and stop replying.
- If Engineering pushes back, forward the email to the VP with "+ looping in."
